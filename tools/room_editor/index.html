ROOM EDITOR

Build mode

  • Drag a thumbnail off the shelf, or any thing in the room by its pixels. Release to drop. Right-click or Esc cancels.
  • Over a top (desk, shelf, fridge…) it lights and the thing stands on it; the drop parents it there. Off a top it sits on the floor.
  • Snap (G, or hold Alt for free): half cells on the floor, quarter cells on tops; walls slide in quarter cells and 4 px.
  • Feet refuse to cross: pushed into a neighbour a thing slides along it until they touch; still crossing shows red.
  • Wall things (posters, clocks) ride the wall you point at, along and up it.
  • Click selects: the panel on the right edits it. Delete, Ctrl+D copy, R flip, Z / X layer, 7 / 8 height, ← → pixel nudge, [ ] frame, Shift+wheel scale.
  • While dragging: R flips, 7 / 8 lift and lower, ← → nudge, [ ] frame.
  • Wheel zooms at the pointer; middle-drag, right-drag or Space+drag pans; Fit recentres.
  • Save (Ctrl+S) writes data/room_layout.lua; Lua shows it. Undo is Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
